## 🎯 Key Concepts

### What is OSINT?
- **Open-Source Intelligence**: Data obtained from publicly available sources
- Used by law enforcement, cybercriminals, businesses for various purposes
- Critical for both red (offensive) and blue (defensive) teams

### OSINT Data Examples
- Company employee pages and contact information
- Job descriptions revealing internal systems (Windows Server, Solaris)
- Geotagged social media photos with metadata
- Social media profiles (DOB, locations, interests, family)
- Cybercriminal identity exposure through poor OPSEC

---

## 📝 Notes

### Why OSINT is Useful

**For Defenders (Blue Team Focus):**
- Conduct **public exposure assessments** to reduce attack surface
- Identify information leakage that aids attackers
- Implement security controls and user awareness training
- Monitor social media for employee information exposure
- Find internet-facing assets and old login portals

**For Attackers (Know Your Enemy):**
- **Target information gathering** (passive reconnaissance)
- Build employee profiles for social engineering
- Identify internal systems for exploit planning
- Craft spear-phishing campaigns tailored to targets

**For Law Enforcement:**
- Track persons of interest and missing persons
- Profile criminal behavior and predict locations
- Uncover cybercriminal identities through poor OPSEC
- Support organizations like Trace Labs (OSINT CTFs for missing persons)

**For Businesses:**
- Competitive intelligence and market monitoring
- Customer engagement analysis
- Monitor for security risks (leaked credentials, confidential info)
- Social media strategy improvement

### Associated Security Roles

**Threat Intelligence:**
- **Tactical Analysts**: Adversary tracking, IOC collection, trend analysis
- **Strategic Analysts**: Threat exposure assessments, information leakage identification

**Security Operations:**
- **Security Analysts**: IOC reputation checking (VirusTotal, IPVoid), fake account investigation
- **Vulnerability Analysts**: CVE monitoring, researcher tracking, NVD usage

**Offensive Security:**
- **Penetration Testers**: Target reconnaissance, attack tailoring, noise reduction
