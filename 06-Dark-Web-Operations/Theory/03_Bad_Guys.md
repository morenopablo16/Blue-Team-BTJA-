
# 📝 03_Bad_Guys

**Module**: Dark Web Operations | **Date**: 2025-09-27

---

## 🎯 Lesson: Illegal sites — quick summary

Illegal marketplaces sell contraband, services, and stolen data. Below are the main categories, how they work, risks, and quick defensive notes.

---

## Main categories (one-liners)

- Drugs and controlled substances
- Weapons and weapon components
- Stolen data and credential dumps
- Malware, botnets, and exploit tooling
- Fraud services, money laundering, and forged documents
- Exploitative content and trafficking-related services

---

## Typical operations (condensed)

- Marketplace UX: listings, vendor pages, ratings, escrow.
- Payments via cryptocurrency; escrow to manage trust.
- Some markets require invitation/vetting; OPSEC is common (PGP, aliases, TOR hidden services).

---

## Top risks for analysts

- Legal exposure from active engagement or handling contraband
- Malware and drive-by infections from downloads
- Scams and fraud; poor reliability of vendors
- Deanonymization or targeting by adversaries or law enforcement

---

## Quick defensive takeaways

- Prefer passive monitoring and metadata collection
- Use isolated, ephemeral VMs and snapshots
- Avoid purchases; collect indicators (hashes, addresses) only
- Correlate findings into TI pipelines and notify legal/LE when needed

---

## Short references

- Case studies: Silk Road, AlphaBay, Hansa/Onymous
