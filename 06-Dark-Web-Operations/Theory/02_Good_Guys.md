**Module**: Dark Web Operations | **Date**: 2025-09-27

## 🎯 Lesson: What Can Be Achieved Legally?

Legitimate activities exist, but laws vary — always consult legal counsel and coordinate with law enforcement when needed.

---

## Legal activities (what defenders can do)

Note: laws differ by country — always consult legal counsel and notify law enforcement when appropriate before conducting dark web operations.

- Passive collection: monitor/scrape public dark web pages and marketplaces for intelligence.
- Lawful private access: analyze content only if access is legitimately obtained (invitation/purchase) and documented.
- Controlled undercover work: use aliases only with legal oversight and strict recording of actions.

---

## Illegal activities to avoid

The following are examples of actions that are likely illegal and must be avoided by security professionals:

Illegal to avoid (short):
- Supplying contraband (malware, stolen data) to gain access.
- Unauthorized access (brute force, exploits, using stolen creds).
- Assisting crimes (selling tools, advice, money).

Consult legal counsel before risky activities.

---

## Law enforcement — short examples

- Operation Onymous: multi‑country takedown of marketplaces (Silk Road era). Seizures and arrests.
- Hansa infiltration: controlled takeover to collect evidence and deanonymize users; led to arrests and seizures.

---

## Threat intelligence firms (concise)

- Monitor and scrape public and private sources for indicators (hashes, creds, bitcoin addresses).
- Gain lawful access to private forums when possible; extract and enrich data.
- Produce alerts, IOCs and context for customers (e.g., Recorded Future).

---

## Key benefits

- Early warning and prioritization
- Detection of leaked credentials and malware indicators
- Context to tune SIEM/EDR and support incident response

---

## Legal & ethical notes

- Consult legal counsel before active/undercover operations.
- Document methods and approvals.
- Coordinate with law enforcement when criminal activity is discovered.
